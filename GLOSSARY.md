# Agentic Flow Kit

AI-Native workflow engine installer and zero-trust governance toolkit for software repositories.

## Language

**Governance Probe**:
A read-only inspection unit that evaluates one governance domain of a target project and yields a four-state status (`passed`, `warned`, `failed`, or `skipped`) alongside diagnostic evidence.
_Avoid_: Health check, guard, guardian

**Doctor Check**:
A single diagnostic row reported during an interactive or JSON environment health inspection of a target project.
_Avoid_: Probe, gate

**Gate Verdict**:
The aggregate merge-readiness decision (`APPROVED` or `REJECTED`) computed across active governance probes under standard or strict enforcement.
_Avoid_: Health status, doctor result
